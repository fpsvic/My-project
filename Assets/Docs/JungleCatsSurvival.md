# Jungle Cats Survival

Open `Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity` in the Unity desktop editor and press Play in the Game view. The scene includes a `Jungle Cats Game Bootstrap` object that builds a 3D jungle survival prototype around two wild cat characters:

- **Toe**: a playable tuxedo cat.
- **Seven**: a brown spotted tabby companion who follows Toe, hunts when hungry, and helps fight or scare predators.

## Controls

- **WASD / Arrow keys**: move Toe
- **Space**: pounce/attack nearby prey or predators
- **Shift**: hiss to scare nearby predators away
- **R**: restart after both cats are overwhelmed

## Goal

Keep Toe and Seven alive as long as possible. Hunt mice and rabbits to refill hunger, avoid starving, and fight or scare away panthers, foxes, hyenas, and wolf packs.


## Preview notes

This is a Unity scene, so it previews through the Unity desktop editor rather than a browser-style static preview. If the scene view looks mostly empty before Play, that is expected: the jungle, cats, predators, prey, camera, and HUD are generated when Play starts.
